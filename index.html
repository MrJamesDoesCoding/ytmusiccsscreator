Install a theme

  1. Add the free Stylus extension to Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  2. Click Install with Stylus on any theme, then confirm in the Stylus tab that opens.
  3. Open music.youtube.com. To switch off a theme, toggle it in the Stylus menu.

Share your own

  1. Build it in the theme maker and give it a name.
  2. Choose Share. GitHub opens with your theme file filled in; sign in and propose the change.
  3. Once the change is merged, your theme appears in this gallery for everyone.
Remaster is a community project and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. Themes change only how the site looks in your browser.
